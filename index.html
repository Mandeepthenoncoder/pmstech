      FAQ

      Questions people ask us first

      What is the free review?

      Tell us about one task that eats your team's time. We write back with a clear answer: can AI do it, how we would set it up and roughly what it would cost. It is free and you do not have to go ahead.

      What does it cost to work with you?

      The review is free. After it, we give you a fixed price in writing for a small test, so you know the full cost before you start. Websites and branding are also quoted as a fixed price up front.

      How fast will we see something working?

      The small test takes 3 to 4 weeks and ends with something real that you can try on your own business. A full build usually takes 6 to 10 weeks after that.

      Will the AI do things without asking us?

      Not for anything important. Payments, refunds, discounts and promises to customers always wait for a person you choose. The AI gets the work ready and then waits.

      Is our data safe?

      The AI only sees what it needs for the task, and everything it does is recorded. We follow India's data protection law, the DPDP Rules, including the parts that start on 13 November 2026 and 13 May 2027.

      Do we need a tech team?

      No. We set everything up, connect it to the tools you already use and train your staff. If you do have a tech person, we work with them.

      Who owns what you build?

      You do. The code, the designs, the brand files, the data and the how to guide are all yours. You can run it yourself at any time.

      What can a voice agent do?

      It answers your phone, replies to common questions, books appointments, takes orders and passes the caller to a person when needed. Anything about money or special promises waits for your approval.
