Privacy policy

What we collect, and what we do with it

This site sets no cookies and runs no trackers. The enquiry form on the home page opens your own email app, so nothing is stored here.

Job applications are different. When you apply through our careers pages we store the name, phone number, email address and answers you give us, so that we can assess your application and contact you about it. We use them for nothing else, and we do not sell or share them. Your answers are scored against what the role needs; a person reads every application before any decision is made.

You can ask us to see or delete your application at any time by writing to careers@purplemagicstudio.com. We delete applications twelve months after they are submitted.

For any other privacy question, write to info@purplemagicstudio.com.

A fuller policy covering our obligations under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act is being prepared ahead of the dates it takes effect.
